## 3. Notes on how the terms apply to this repository

These notes are explanatory only. `LICENSE.txt` is the authoritative text; if
the two ever disagree, `LICENSE.txt` governs.

**Is this open source?**
No. The source is *visible* and *studyable*, which is different from *open
source*. You may learn from it, and you may modify it for yourself. You may not
republish the pack or a derivative of it on a monetised platform without written
permission.

**May I include Muon in a modpack?**
Yes, including a commercial modpack, as long as the pack is credited and the
copyright notice is preserved.

**May I put a modified Muon on a shader sharing site?**
Only if that platform does not provide direct or indirect monetary compensation
to creators. Modrinth and CurseForge are the two named examples in the licence.
Sites without creator payouts are permitted under the same "redistribute
modified portions" clause, provided the notice is kept.

**May I use Muon in a YouTube video or a Twitch stream?**
Yes. Monetised videos and streams are explicitly permitted; credit is required.

**May I sell a resource pack that bundles my Muon edits?**
No. That is "demanding money in exchange for copies of Muon or derivative
works", which requires written permission.

**What about the noise textures in `shaders/image/`?**
They are generated by `tools/gen_noise.py` and are part of Muon, so the same
terms apply.

**Third-party references**
Muon was developed by studying the published techniques in other shader packs
(Photon by SixthSurge, SEUS PTGI by Sonic Ether). No code from those packs is
included. `docs/architecture.md` records which techniques were studied and how
Muon's implementation differs.
